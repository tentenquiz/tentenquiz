Places / Transport Word Dictionary

The places and transport section starts with basic units of a location, like place, city, and village, then expands into specific spaces within a city, like commercial districts, tourist attractions, and observation decks, as the stages go up. It goes from asking for directions to describing what kind of place somewhere actually is. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.
