Places / Transport Word Dictionary

The places and transport section starts with basic units of a location, like place, city, and village, then expands into specific spaces within a city, like commercial districts, tourist attractions, and observation decks, as the stages go up. It goes from asking for directions to describing what kind of place somewhere actually is. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.

Stage 1

lugar—place

A particular area where someone or something is located.

ciudad—city

A large place where many people live and work.

localidad—town

A settled place smaller than a city and larger than a village.

aldea—village

A small community in the countryside.

país—country

A land with its own government and borders.

carretera—road

A wide way that vehicles use to travel between places.

calle—street

A public road in a city or town with buildings along it.

sendero—path

A narrow way for people to walk along.

puente—bridge

A structure that carries a way over water or another obstacle.

estación—station

A place where trains stop for passengers.

aeropuerto—airport

A place where airplanes take off and land.

puerto—port

A place by water where ships arrive and leave.

escuela—school

A place where students learn with teachers.

hospital—hospital

A place where sick or injured people receive care.

tienda—store

A place where goods are sold.

parque—park

A public outdoor area with trees and space for rest or play.

entrada—entrance

The place used to go into a building or area.

salida—exit

The place used to leave a building or area.

coche—car

A road vehicle with an engine, usually for a small number of people.

tren—train

A group of connected vehicles that travels on rails.

bicicleta—bicycle

A vehicle with two wheels moved by pedals.

avión—airplane

A powered vehicle with wings that flies through the air.

barco—ship

A large vehicle used to travel on water.

conductor—driver

A person who controls a road vehicle.

pasajero—passenger

A person traveling in a vehicle but not driving it.

Stage 2

ir—go

To move from one place to another.

venir—come

To move toward the speaker or a named place.

partir—depart

To begin a journey by leaving a place.

llegar—arrive

To reach the place at the end of a journey.

entrar—enter

To move from outside to inside a place.

salir—go out

To move from inside a place to the outside.

cruzar—cross

To move from one side of something to the other.

girar—turn

To change the direction of movement.

detenerse—stop

To no longer move or continue.

esperar—wait

To stay until someone arrives or something happens.

conducir—drive

To control and move a road vehicle.

subir—board

To get onto a vehicle for travel.

bajar—get off

To leave a vehicle after traveling in it.

hacer transbordo—transfer

To leave one vehicle and continue on another.

pasar—pass

To move by or beyond a person or place.

regresar—return

To go back to a place where one was before.

desplazarse—move

To change position or go to another place.

viajar—travel

To go from place to place, often over a long distance.

acercarse—approach

To move nearer to a person or place.

transportar—carry

To move a person or thing from one place to another.

volar—fly

To move through the air.

navegar—sail

To travel on water in a ship or boat.

retroceder—reverse

To move backward instead of forward.

desplazarse a diario—commute

To travel regularly between home and work or school.

desviarse—detour

To take a different route around a blocked or difficult way.

Stage 3

edificio—building

A structure with walls and a roof where people live or work.

oficina—office

A room or building where people do desk work.

banco—bank

A place that keeps money and provides financial services.

oficina de correos—post office

A place where letters and packages are sent and received.

biblioteca—library

A place where books can be read or borrowed.

comisaría—police station

A building where police officers work.

estación de bomberos—fire station

A building where firefighters and their vehicles are based.

farmacia—pharmacy

A place where medicines are prepared and sold.

mercado—market

A place where many sellers offer food or other goods.

museo—museum

A place that keeps and displays important objects.

teatro—theater

A building where plays and performances are shown.

playa—beach

A sandy or stony area beside the sea.

bosque—forest

A large area covered mainly with trees.

isla—island

A piece of land completely surrounded by water.

lago—lake

A large area of water surrounded by land.

mar—sea

A very large body of salt water.

centro urbano—downtown

The central part of a city with many shops and offices.

suburbio—suburb

An area where people live outside the center of a city.

barrio—neighborhood

The area around the place where someone lives.

paso de peatones—crosswalk

A marked part of a road where people can cross safely.

acera—sidewalk

A path beside a road for people to walk on.

semáforo—traffic light

A set of colored lights that controls road traffic.

intersección—intersection

A place where two or more roads meet or cross.

aparcamiento—parking lot

An area where vehicles can be left for a time.

ferrocarril—railway

A system of rails used by trains.

The direction toward the top of most maps.

sur—south

The direction toward the bottom of most maps.

este—east

The direction where the sun rises.

oeste—west

The direction where the sun sets.

izquierda—left

The side opposite the right side.

derecha—right

The side opposite the left side.

frente—front

The part or side that faces forward.

parte trasera—back

The part or side opposite the front.

interior—inside

The inner part of a place or object.

exterior—outside

The outer part or area beyond a place or object.

alrededores—vicinity

The area near a particular place.

distancia—distance

The amount of space between two places or things.

dirección—direction

The line or way in which someone or something moves or faces.

ruta—route

A way followed to get from one place to another.

atajo—shortcut

A shorter way to reach a place.

esquina—corner

The point where two sides, walls, or streets meet.

frontera—border

A line that separates two areas or countries.

centro—center

The middle point or part of an area or object.

zona—area

A particular part of a larger place.

región—region

A large area with shared features or boundaries.

dirección postal—address

The written details that show where a place is.

ubicación—location

The exact place where someone or something is.

destino—destination

The place a person or vehicle is traveling to.

punto de partida—starting point

The place where a journey or route begins.

punto final—end point

The place where a route or line ends.

Stage 5

transporte—transportation

The system or activity of moving people and goods between places.

vehículo—vehicle

A machine used to carry people or goods from place to place.

metro—subway

A city railway that runs mainly underground.

tranvía—tram

A passenger vehicle that runs on rails along city streets.

ambulancia—ambulance

A vehicle that takes sick or injured people for urgent care.

camión de bomberos—fire engine

A vehicle carrying firefighters and equipment to fires.

tren expreso—express train

A train that stops at fewer stations to travel faster.

tren local—local train

A train that stops at most or all stations on its route.

billete—ticket

A paper or digital pass that lets someone use public transportation.

tarifa—fare

The money paid for a journey on transportation.

horario—timetable

A list showing the planned times of transport services.

retraso—delay

A situation in which something happens later than planned.

velocidad—speed

How fast something moves.

atasco—traffic jam

A line of slow or stopped vehicles on a crowded road.

accidente—accident

An unexpected event that causes damage or injury.

señal vial—road sign

A sign beside a road that gives information or rules.

carril—lane

A marked strip of road for one line of vehicles.

autopista—highway

A major road designed for fast travel over long distances.

vía férrea—track

A pair of rails that trains travel on.

combustible—fuel

A material burned or used to produce energy for movement.

rueda—wheel

A round part that turns and helps a vehicle move.

asiento—seat

A place made for one person to sit.

equipaje—baggage

Bags and cases carried by a traveler.

viaje—journey

The act or period of traveling from one place to another.

peatón—pedestrian

A person traveling on foot rather than in a vehicle.

Stage 6

cercano—near

A short distance away from a place.

lejano—far

A long distance away from a place.

recto—straight

Continuing in one direction without bending.

concurrido—crowded

Filled with too many people or vehicles.

vacío—empty

Containing no people or things.

ancho—wide

Having a large distance from one side to the other.

estrecho—narrow

Having a small distance from one side to the other.

limpio—clean

Free from dirt or waste.

sucio—dirty

Covered with dirt or waste.

tranquilo—quiet

Making little or no noise.

seguro—safe

Not likely to cause harm or danger.

peligroso—dangerous

Likely to cause harm or injury.

rápido—fast

Moving or happening at high speed.

lento—slow

Moving or happening at low speed.

local—local

Belonging to or connected with a nearby area.

directo—direct

Going to a destination without changing vehicles.

público—public

Open or intended for everyone to use.

privado—private

Owned or used by one person or a limited group.

subterráneo—underground

Located below the surface of the ground.

en superficie—aboveground

Located on or above the surface of the ground.

accesible—accessible

Easy to reach or enter.

conveniente—convenient

Easy and useful for a particular need.

inconveniente—inconvenient

Difficult or unsuitable for a particular need.

abierto—open

Able to be entered or used.

cerrado—closed

Not able to be entered or used at the time.

reserva—reservation

An arrangement made in advance to keep a place or service.

cancelación—cancellation

The act of deciding that a planned service or event will not happen.

confirmación—confirmation

Information showing that an arrangement is accepted or correct.

viaje de ida y vuelta—round trip

A journey to a place and back to the starting point.

viaje de ida—one-way trip

A journey to a destination without a return included.

conexión—connection

A transport service used after another to continue a journey.

hora de salida—departure time

The planned time when a journey or service begins.

hora de llegada—arrival time

The planned time when a journey or service reaches its destination.

tiempo de viaje—travel time

The amount of time needed to complete a journey.

brújula—compass

A tool with a needle that shows direction.

punto de referencia—landmark

A noticeable place or object used to recognize a location.

poste indicador—signpost

A sign that points toward places or routes.

puesto de control—checkpoint

A place where people or vehicles are stopped for inspection.

aduana—customs

The official check of people entering a country.

paso a nivel—railroad crossing

A place where a road crosses railway tracks.

A place where drivers pay to use a road.

área de descanso—rest area

A place beside a road where travelers can stop and rest.

obras viales—roadwork

Work done to build or repair a road.

obra en construcción—construction site

A place where a building or structure is being built.

ruta de desvío—detour route

An alternate route around a blocked or difficult road.

cierre de carretera—road closure

A situation in which a road is blocked and cannot be used.

control de tráfico—traffic control

The management of vehicles and people to keep traffic moving safely.

límite de velocidad—speed limit

The highest speed legally allowed on a road.

tarifa de estacionamiento—parking fee

Money paid for leaving a vehicle in a parking space.

consulado—consulate

fábrica—factory

estadio—stadium

A large place with seats for watching sports.

plaza—square

An open public space in a town or city.

monumento—monument

A structure built to remember a person or event.

Water falling from a high place in a river or stream.

cueva—cave

A natural hollow space in the ground or a mountain.

A place where water naturally comes out of the ground.

campo—field

An open area of land with grass or crops.

canal—canal

A man-made waterway for boats or moving water.

A secure building where people are kept as legal punishment.

paso subterráneo—underpass

A passage that goes beneath a road or railway.

puente peatonal—footbridge

A bridge made for people walking rather than vehicles.

A road or passage with no way out at one end.

escalera—staircase

A set of steps used to move between different levels.

valla—fence

A barrier around an area made of posts, wire, or boards.

portón—gate

A movable barrier that controls entry through a fence or wall.

continente—continent

One of the world’s very large continuous areas of land.

península—peninsula

Land almost surrounded by water but joined to larger land.

archipiélago—archipelago

A group of islands located near one another.

cabo—cape

A point of land that extends into the sea.

A narrow stretch of water connecting two larger bodies of water.

llanura—plain

A large area of mostly flat land.

Low land surrounded by higher ground.

The land along the edge of a river.

A high steep wall of rock or earth.

revisor—conductor

A worker who helps passengers and checks tickets on a train.

empleado de estación—station attendant

A worker who helps passengers and manages tasks at a station.

capitán—captain

The person in command of a ship.

An area made up mainly of homes where people live.

An area where many stores and businesses are gathered.

An area where many factories and industrial buildings are located.

sitio histórico—historical site

A place where buildings or remains connected to history are preserved.

atracción turística—tourist attraction

A well-known place that many travelers visit and see.

A raised place made for looking at the surrounding view.

zona protegida—protected area

An area set aside to protect nature, animals, or plants.

A counter where people can ask for directions or help in a public place.

A place set aside for leaving bicycles.

zona peatonal—pedestrian zone

An area set aside for people to walk without regular car traffic.

calle de sentido único—one-way street

A street where vehicles may travel in only one direction.
