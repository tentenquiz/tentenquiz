Activities / Leisure Word Dictionary

The activities and leisure section starts with things you can do alone, like play, read, and draw, then expands into words about culture and doing things together, like heritage, participation, and cooperation, as the stages go up. It moves from naming a hobby to talking about shared activities and culture. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.
