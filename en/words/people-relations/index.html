People / Relationships Word Dictionary

The people and relations section starts with words for close family and friends, then expands into more abstract social words like empathy, prejudice, reputation, and influence as the stages go up. Early stages name who someone is; later stages describe how people relate to, judge, and influence one another. Useful for introducing your family as much as for talking about trust or reputation. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.
