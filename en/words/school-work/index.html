School / Work Word Dictionary

The school and work section starts with everyday classroom words like school, student, and teacher, then expands into career and workplace words like training, career, department, and role as the stages go up. It moves from describing a class to talking about your job or career path. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.

Stage 1

escuela—school

A place where students go to learn.

estudiante—student

A person who learns at a school or from a teacher.

profesor—teacher

A person whose job is to help others learn.

lección—lesson

A period of time when a teacher teaches students.

aula—classroom

A room where students have lessons.

pupitre—desk

A table used for reading, writing, or studying.

A piece of furniture made for one person to sit on.

libro—book

Pages with writing or pictures joined together for reading.

papel—paper

A thin flat material used for writing, drawing, or printing.

lápiz—pencil

A tool with a graphite center used for writing or drawing.

regla—ruler

A straight tool used to measure length or draw lines.

pizarra—blackboard

A large dark board that a teacher writes on during a lesson.

tarea—homework

Schoolwork that a student must do outside class.

pregunta—question

Something asked because a person wants an answer or information.

respuesta—answer

Words or information given in response to a question.

aprender—learn

To gain knowledge or a skill through study or experience.

enseñar—teach

To help someone gain knowledge or a skill.

leer—read

To look at written words and understand their meaning.

escribir—write

To make letters or words on a surface.

escuchar—listen

To pay attention to a sound or to someone speaking.

hablar—speak

To use the voice to express words or ideas.

practicar—practice

To do something repeatedly in order to improve.

recordar—remember

To keep information in the mind and not forget it.

olvidar—forget

To fail to remember something that was known.

entender—understand

To know what something means or how it works.

To make an idea clear by giving information about it.

repetir—repeat

comparar—compare

To look at two or more things to see how they are alike or different.

resolver—solve

To find the answer to a question or problem.

comprobar—check

To examine something to make sure it is right or complete.

elegir—choose

To decide which person or thing to take from several options.

copiar—copy

To write the same words that are shown somewhere else.

dibujar—draw

To make a picture with lines or colors.

calcular—calculate

To use numbers to find an amount or result.

correcto—correct

Having no mistake and matching the right answer or fact.

incorrecto—wrong

Not correct or not matching the true answer or fact.

fácil—easy

Requiring little effort or skill to do or understand.

difícil—difficult

Requiring much effort, thought, or skill to do or understand.

examen—test

A set of questions or tasks used to check what someone has learned.

calificación—grade

A mark that shows how well a student performed on schoolwork.

asignatura—subject

An area of knowledge taught at school, such as history or science.

A system of words and rules that people use to communicate.

palabra—word

A single unit of language that has meaning.

oración—sentence

A group of words that expresses a complete idea.

significado—meaning

The idea that a word, sign, or action expresses.

ejemplo—example

A specific case used to make a larger group or idea easier to understand.

diccionario—dictionary

A book or resource that gives the meanings of words.

The gate used to enter or leave school grounds.

pasillo—hallway

A long passage inside a building that connects rooms.

aula de ciencias—science room

A classroom prepared for learning science and doing simple experiments.

aula de música—music room

A classroom used for learning, singing, and playing music.

aula de arte—art room

A classroom used for drawing, painting, and making art.

sala de profesores—staff room

A room at school where teachers work and prepare lessons.

A building where students live while attending school.

A book made for learning a particular school subject.

A table showing which lessons happen at each time.

capítulo—chapter

A main section of a book that is divided from other sections.

título—title

The name given to a book, text, picture, or other work.

párrafo—paragraph

A group of sentences about one main idea in a text.

línea—line

A row of written or printed words across a page.

símbolo—symbol

A mark or shape used to represent an idea or information.

mapa—map

A drawing that shows the locations of places and roads.

dibujo—drawing

An image made by hand with lines, shapes, or colors.

An image of a real scene captured with a camera.

A school group whose members share an activity or interest.

A short break between school lessons.

A formal event held for an important occasion.

graduación—graduation

The completion of a course of study at a school.

excursión escolar—field trip

tarea asignada—task

investigación—research

grupo—group

permiso—permission

aviso—notice

A short message that gives important information.

entregar—submit

pedir prestado—borrow

prestar—lend

discutir—discuss

To organize and control work, people, or resources.

To make something better than it was before.

cambiar—change

reparar—repair

enviar—send

compartir—share

Activity that uses effort or knowledge to achieve a result.

profesión—job

The kind of work a person regularly does to earn a living.

trabajador—worker

A person who works to produce goods or provide services.

empresa—company

An organization where people work to provide goods or services.

oficina—office

A room or place where administrative work is done.

lugar de trabajo—workplace

The place where a person regularly does a job.

A person who directs the work of other employees.

colega—colleague

A person who works in the same place or organization as another.

A person paid to work for an organization.

gerente—manager

A person who organizes and controls people or work.

reunión—meeting

A gathering where people discuss work or make decisions.

A room prepared for people to hold meetings.

A decision made in advance about what to do and how to do it.

An arranged list of activities and the times they will happen.

A written or printed record that contains information.

A document with spaces where required information is written.

firma—signature

A person’s written name used to show approval or identity.

currículum—résumé

A short document that lists a person’s education, skills, and work experience.

salario—salary

Money regularly paid to a person for their work.

An official day when schools and many workplaces are closed.

A formal conversation in which questions are asked to judge a person’s suitability.

solicitud—application

A formal request for a school place, a job, or entry to a program.

A formal agreement that states what each side must do.

inútil—useless

Following official rules or accepted ways for an important situation.

Able to act or work without relying on others.

The power or capacity to do something.

conocimiento—knowledge

Information and understanding gained through learning or experience.

experiencia—experience

Knowledge or skill gained by doing or seeing things directly.

opinión—opinion

A series of stages through which something is done or changes.

opción—option

One of several things that can be chosen.

solución—solution

A way to fix a problem or difficulty.

desafío—challenge

responsabilidad—responsibility

A duty to take care of something and answer for the result.

consejo—advice

An opinion given to help someone decide what to do.

instrucción—instruction

A statement that tells someone what to do or how to do it.

Facts or details that help someone know about something.

resumen—summary

A short statement of the main points of a longer text or talk.

lista—list

A set of names or items written one after another.

registro—record

Stored written information about an event or activity.

hecho—fact

prueba—evidence

Information or an object that helps show something is true.

referencia—reference

anuncio—announcement

A public message that gives information to many people.

orden del día—agenda

A list of subjects to be discussed at a meeting.

acta—meeting minutes

A written record of what was discussed and decided in a meeting.

A formal discussion in which people present different views on a subject.

A final judgment reached after considering information.

A quality or ability that is less developed or needs improvement.

condición—condition

A general rule or direction followed by an organization.

procedimiento—procedure

A fixed series of steps used to do something correctly.

advertencia—warning

A message that tells people about possible danger or trouble.

riesgo—risk

The possibility that something harmful or unwanted may happen.

The process of teaching and learning knowledge, skills, and values.

Practice and instruction given to improve a skill or prepare for work.

The path of jobs and work that a person follows over time.

examen de ingreso—entrance exam

A test given at the end of a school term or course.

A school event in which students take part in games and races.

ceremonia de graduación—graduation ceremony

A student chosen to represent a class and take on some class duties.

premio—prize

seguridad—safety

The state of being protected from danger or harm.
