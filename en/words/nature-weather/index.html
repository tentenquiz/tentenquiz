Nature / Weather Word Dictionary

The nature and weather section starts with everyday sky and weather words like sun, moon, and rain, then expands into large-scale natural events like earthquakes, tsunamis, and volcanic eruptions as the stages go up. It goes from small talk about the weather to the vocabulary needed to follow news about natural disasters. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.
