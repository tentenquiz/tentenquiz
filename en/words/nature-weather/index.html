Nature / Weather Word Dictionary

The nature and weather section starts with everyday sky and weather words like sun, moon, and rain, then expands into large-scale natural events like earthquakes, tsunamis, and volcanic eruptions as the stages go up. It goes from small talk about the weather to the vocabulary needed to follow news about natural disasters. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.

Stage 1

The bright star that gives Earth light and heat.

luna—moon

The round natural object seen in the night sky around Earth.

estrella—star

A distant point of light seen in the night sky.

cielo—sky

The open space seen above the ground.

A visible mass of tiny water drops or ice in the sky.

Water drops that fall from clouds.

Soft white ice crystals that fall from clouds.

Air moving across the ground or through the sky.

montaña—mountain

A very high natural area of land with steep sides.

A large natural stream of water flowing across land.

A vast area of salt water covering much of Earth.

lago—lake

A large area of water surrounded by land.

A large area where many trees grow close together.

A tall plant with a hard trunk and branches.

The colorful part of a plant that often produces seeds.

A common short green plant with narrow leaves.

The loose upper layer of ground where plants grow.

piedra—stone

A small hard solid piece naturally found on the ground.

Very small loose grains of rock found on beaches and ground.

An area of land completely surrounded by water.

colina—hill

A raised area of land lower and gentler than a mountain.

The warmest season of the year.

The season after summer when weather cools and many leaves change color.

invierno—winter

The coldest season of the year.

marchitarse—wither

bambú—bamboo

A tall plant with hollow jointed stems.

Light that reaches Earth from the sun.

sombra—shadow

A dark shape formed when light is blocked.

arcoíris—rainbow

A curved band of colors that can appear in the sky after rain.

niebla—fog

A thick layer of tiny water drops near the ground that makes seeing difficult.

rocío—dew

Small drops of water that form on cool surfaces outdoors.

escarcha—frost

A thin white layer of ice crystals that forms on cold surfaces.

hielo—ice

Water that has frozen into a solid form.

trueno—thunder

The loud sound heard after lightning.

A sudden bright flash of electricity in the sky.

llovizna—drizzle

Very light rain made of fine drops.

chubasco—rain shower

Rain that begins suddenly and usually ends soon.

granizo—hail

tornado—tornado

A violently rotating column of air extending from a storm cloud.

sequía—drought

A long period with too little rain and not enough water.

inundación—flood

A large amount of water covering land that is normally dry.

cielo despejado—clear sky

A sky with little or no cloud and good visibility.

cielo nublado—cloudy sky

humedad—humidity

temperatura del aire—air temperature

A statement about weather expected in the future.

cascada—waterfall

Water dropping from a high place in a river or stream.

manantial—natural spring

A place where water naturally flows out of the ground.

estanque—pond

A small area of still water surrounded by land.

pantano—swamp

Wet soft land covered partly by shallow water.

arroyo—stream

A small narrow flow of water across land.

costa—coast

The land along the edge of the sea.

playa—beach

A sandy or stony area beside the sea or a lake.

ola—wave

A raised moving line of water on the surface of the sea or a lake.

marea—tide

The regular rise and fall of sea level.

desierto—desert

A very dry area with little rain and few plants.

llanura—plain

A large area of mostly flat land.

meseta—plateau

A broad flat area of land at a high elevation.

volcán—volcano

cañón—canyon

A deep narrow valley with steep sides.

glaciar—glacier

A huge mass of ice that moves slowly over land.

continente—continent

One of Earth's very large continuous areas of land.

The solid dry surface of Earth rather than the sea.

The distant line where the land or sea appears to meet the sky.

bahía—bay

A curved part of a sea or lake partly surrounded by land.

cabo—cape

The moon when its visible face appears completely round.

A recognized shape or group formed by stars in the sky.

An event in which the moon blocks some or all of the sun from view.

An event in which Earth's shadow darkens the moon.

Very heavy rain falling in a short time or over an area.

A large amount of snow falling strongly or accumulating deeply.

A light and gentle wind.

ráfaga—gust

A sudden short burst of strong wind.

copo de nieve—snowflake

A single soft crystal or cluster of crystals falling as snow.

gota de lluvia—raindrop

A large round object that travels around a star.

galaxia—galaxy

A huge group of stars, gas, and dust held together in space.

A small icy object that can form a bright tail near the sun.

A small rocky object that travels around the sun.

órbita—orbit

The curved path followed by an object moving around another object in space.

A human-made object that travels around Earth or another body in space.

atmósfera—atmosphere

The layer of gases surrounding Earth or another planet.

The mixture of gases surrounding us that living things use to breathe.

roca—rock

A large hard natural mass of stone.

grava—gravel

Small loose pieces of stone covering the ground.

arcilla—clay

Soft earth that becomes sticky when wet and hard when baked.

mineral—mineral

A natural solid substance found in rocks and the ground.

cristal—crystal

fósil—fossil

concha marina—seashell

The hard outer covering of a shellfish.

coral—coral

A hard branching structure made by tiny sea animals.

A large underwater formation built mainly from coral.

corriente oceánica—ocean current

A steady flow of seawater moving through the ocean.

agua subterránea—groundwater

Water stored or flowing beneath the ground.

agua dulce—freshwater

Water with very little salt, such as water in rivers and lakes.

agua de mar—seawater

The salty water found in seas and oceans.

vapor de agua—water vapor

Water in the form of an invisible gas in the air.

agua de lluvia—rainwater

Water that falls as rain and collects on the ground or in containers.

anfibio—amphibian

An animal that usually lives both in water and on land during its life.

pez—fish

Animals that live in water and breathe mainly through gills.

herbívoro—herbivore

An animal that eats mainly plants.

carnívoro—carnivore

An animal that eats mainly other animals.

omnívoro—omnivore

An animal that eats both plants and other animals.

An animal that hunts and eats other animals.

presa—prey

An animal hunted and eaten by another animal.

migración animal—animal migration

The regular movement of animals from one region to another.

hibernación—hibernation

A long sleep-like state that helps some animals survive winter.

cadena alimentaria—food chain

A natural order showing which living things eat others.

ecosistema—ecosystem

especie en peligro—endangered species

A kind of living thing at risk of disappearing completely.

extinción—extinction

The complete disappearance of a kind of living thing.

adaptación—adaptation

A change or ability that helps a living thing survive in its surroundings.

huella—footprint

A mark left on the ground by a foot or paw.

pluma—feather

One of the light coverings that grow on a bird's body.

ala—wing

A body part used by birds and some other animals for flying.

pico—beak

A sharp curved nail on the foot of an animal or bird.

A hard pointed growth on the head of some animals.

A harmful event caused by natural forces.

A sudden shaking of the ground.

A series of very large sea waves caused by movement under the ocean.

The release of hot rock, ash, and gas from a volcano.

Very hot melted rock that flows from a volcano.

ceniza—ash

Fine gray powder left after something burns.

A large uncontrolled fire burning through natural land.

caída de rocas—rockfall

Rock that falls from a cliff or steep slope.

The sudden breaking and falling down of land or a structure.

alerta—alert

An official notice that a dangerous event may happen or is happening.

evacuación—evacuation

Moving away from a dangerous place to safety.

refugio—shelter

A safe place where people stay during danger or disaster.

rescate—rescue

The act of saving someone from danger.

supervivencia—survival

The state of continuing to live despite danger or difficulty.

daño—damage

Harm caused to people, nature, or property.

medio ambiente—environment

The natural and human surroundings in which living things exist.

contaminación—pollution

Harmful or dirty material added to air, water, or land.

basura—garbage

Unwanted material that people throw away.

aguas residuales—wastewater

Dirty water carried away from homes and buildings.

protección ambiental—environmental protection

The act of protecting the environment from harm.

clima—climate

The usual weather pattern of a place over many years.

cambio climático—climate change

A long-term change in usual weather patterns.

calentamiento global—global warming

The long-term rise in Earth's average temperature.
