Food / Drink Word Dictionary

The food and drink section starts with everyday staples like water, rice, and meat, then expands into more specific food and eating-related expressions like shelf life, food waste, and brown rice as the stages go up. It goes beyond ordering at a restaurant, into words used for grocery shopping and managing food at home. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.
