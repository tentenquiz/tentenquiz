Time / Calendar Word Dictionary

The time and calendar section starts with words for the time of day, like morning, afternoon, and noon, then expands into concepts for planning and managing time, like a schedule conflict and sleep duration, as the stages go up. It moves from saying what time it is to talking about coordinating a schedule. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.

Stage 1

tiempo—time

The continuous flow in which events happen and change.

día—daytime

The part of a day when it is light outside.

noche—night

The dark part of a day when most people sleep.

mañana—morning

The early part of a day after waking up.

tarde—afternoon

The part of a day between noon and evening.

mediodía—noon

The middle of the day between morning and afternoon.

hoy—today

The day that is happening now.

día siguiente—next day

The day that comes immediately after a particular day.

ayer—yesterday

The day that came before today.

ahora—now

At the present moment.

luego—later

At a time after the present moment.

temprano—early

Happening before the usual or expected time.

tardío—late

Happening after the usual or expected time.

antes—before

At an earlier time than another event or moment.

después—after

At a later time than another event or moment.

comenzar—begin

To make an action or event start.

terminar—end

To reach the point where an action or event stops.

esperar—wait

To stay until someone arrives or something happens.

despertarse—wake up

To stop sleeping and become awake.

dormir—sleep

To rest with the eyes closed and the body inactive.

reloj de pared—wall clock

A clock fixed to a wall to show the time.

horario—schedule

A plan showing when activities or events will happen.

fecha—date

A particular day identified on a calendar system.

semana—week

A period of seven days used in ordinary schedules.

año—year

A period in which the seasons complete one full cycle.

A short unit of time made up of sixty seconds.

A main calendar period longer than a week and shorter than a year.

estación—season

A part of the year marked by typical weather and natural changes.

The season when the weather becomes warmer and plants grow again.

The warmest season of the year.

The season when the weather cools and many leaves change color.

invierno—winter

The coldest season of the year.

The weekday that comes after Sunday.

The weekday that comes after Monday.

The weekday that comes after Tuesday.

jueves—Thursday

The weekday that comes after Wednesday.

The weekday that comes after Thursday.

The day of the week that comes after Friday.

domingo—Sunday

The day of the week that comes after Saturday.

The part of the week when many people do not work or attend school.

día laborable—weekday

A day of the week when work or school normally takes place.

día libre—day off

A day when a person does not have to work or attend school.

cumpleaños—birthday

The yearly date on which a person was born.

descanso—break

A short period when an activity stops so people can rest.

A longer period away from work or school for rest.

The middle point of the night when a new day begins.

amanecer—dawn

The time when light first appears before sunrise.

anochecer—dusk

The time when daylight fades after sunset.

recientemente—recently

nunca—never

Not at any time or on any occasion.

On some occasions but not regularly.

Not often and only on a few occasions.

a diario—daily

Happening or done every week.

cada mes—monthly

Happening or done every month.

cada año—yearly

Happening or done every year.

The amount of time that an event or activity continues.

recordatorio—reminder

despertador—alarm clock

The usual or chosen time to go to sleep.

Time when no work, study, or required task must be done.

reservar—reserve

llegar—arrive

partir—depart

To leave a place and begin a journey.

volver—return

continuar—continue

darse prisa—hurry

To decide in advance what will be done and when.

preparar—prepare

To make things ready before an activity or event.

organizar—organize

To arrange tasks or events in a clear and useful order.

recordar—remember

To keep or bring information back into the mind.

olvidar—forget

To fail to remember information or something that must be done.

ausencia—absence

The state of not being present at a place or event.

ocupado—busy

Having many tasks and little free time.

permanente—permanent

A yearly date that remembers an important past event.

boda—wedding

graduación—graduation

A preserved account of events that happened in the past.

juventud—youth

edad adulta—adulthood

vejez—old age

The later stage of life when a person has grown old.

vida entera—lifetime

recuerdo—recollection

tradición—tradition

costumbre—custom

irregular—irregular

anterior—previous

actual—current
