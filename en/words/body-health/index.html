Body / Health Word Dictionary

The body and health section starts with body parts like head, eye, and nose, then expands into real medical terms like asthma, diabetes, and anaemia as the stages go up. After learning the parts of the body, you move on to the words you need to describe a health condition. Handy for a doctor's visit or talking about a check-up result. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.

Stage 1

cuerpo—body

The complete physical form of a person.

cabeza—head

The upper part of the body containing the brain and face.

cara—face

The front of the head where the eyes, nose, and mouth are.

ojo—eye

The organ used for seeing.

oreja—ear

The organ used for hearing sounds.

nariz—nose

The part used for breathing and smelling.

boca—mouth

The opening used for eating, speaking, and breathing.

diente—tooth

A hard structure in the mouth used to bite and chew food.

lengua—tongue

The soft part in the mouth used for tasting and speaking.

cuello—neck

The part that connects the head to the rest of the body.

hombro—shoulder

The part where the arm joins the upper body.

brazo—arm

The limb extending from the shoulder to the hand.

mano—hand

The part at the end of the arm used to hold and touch.

dedo—finger

One of the movable parts extending from the hand.

pecho—chest

The front upper part of the body between the neck and stomach.

espalda—back

The rear part of the body from the shoulders downward.

barriga—belly

The front lower part of the body below the chest.

pierna—leg

The limb used for standing and walking.

rodilla—knee

The joint in the middle of the leg that allows it to bend.

cintura—waist

The middle part of the body between the chest and hips.

dedo del pie—toe

One of the small movable parts at the end of the foot.

piel—skin

The outer covering that protects the body.

cabello—hair

The strands that grow from the skin of the head.

corazón—heart

The organ that pumps blood through the body.

sangre—blood

The red liquid that flows through the body and carries needed substances.

The front part of the head above the eyes.

ceja—eyebrow

The line of hair growing above each eye.

párpado—eyelid

The movable skin that opens and closes over the eye.

pestaña—eyelash

One of the short hairs growing along the edge of an eyelid.

mejilla—cheek

The soft side of the face below the eye.

barbilla—chin

The lowest front part of the face below the mouth.

labio—lip

One of the two soft edges around the opening of the mouth.

garganta—throat

The passage inside the neck used for breathing and swallowing.

codo—elbow

The joint in the middle of the arm that allows it to bend.

muñeca—wrist

The joint connecting the hand to the arm.

palma—palm

The inner flat surface of the hand.

pulgar—thumb

The short thick finger set apart from the other fingers.

uña—nail

The hard thin covering at the end of a finger.

muslo—thigh

The upper part of the leg between the hip and knee.

pantorrilla—calf

The fleshy back part of the lower leg.

tobillo—ankle

The joint connecting the foot area to the leg.

talón—heel

The rounded back part at the bottom of the foot.

planta—sole

The lower surface of the foot that touches the ground.

hueso—bone

A hard part inside the body that supports its shape.

músculo—muscle

Body tissue that tightens and relaxes to create movement.

cerebro—brain

The organ in the head that controls thought, feeling, and movement.

pulmón—lung

One of the organs in the chest used for breathing.

hígado—liver

An organ that processes nutrients and removes harmful substances.

riñón—kidney

One of the organs that filters waste from the blood.

estómago—stomach

The organ where food is held and broken down after swallowing.

Stage 3

respirar—breathe

To take air into the lungs and release it again.

parpadear—blink

To close and open the eyelids quickly.

ver—see

To notice light, shapes, or movement with the eyes.

oír—hear

To notice sound through the ears.

tragar—swallow

To move food or liquid from the mouth down the throat.

masticar—chew

To crush food with the teeth before swallowing.

morder—bite

To press or cut something with the teeth.

lamer—lick

To move the tongue across a surface.

dormir—sleep

To rest with the eyes closed and awareness reduced.

despertar—wake

To stop sleeping and become aware again.

estar de pie—stand

To hold the body upright on the feet.

sentarse—sit

To rest the lower body on a surface while upright.

caminar—walk

To move forward by taking steps without running.

correr—run

To move quickly with steps that briefly leave the ground.

saltar—jump

To push the body off the ground into the air.

doblar—bend

To change a straight body part into a curved position.

estirar—stretch

To extend a body part to its full length.

girar—turn

To move the body around toward another direction.

sacudir—shake

To move a body part repeatedly from side to side.

levantar—lift

To raise something or a body part upward.

caer—fall

To lose balance and drop to the ground.

descansar—rest

To stop activity so the body or mind can recover.

gatear—crawl

To move close to the ground using the hands and knees or body.

arrodillarse—kneel

To lower the body so one or both knees touch the ground.

hacer ejercicio—exercise

To move the body deliberately to improve strength or health.

Feeling healthy and in good condition.

enfermo—sick

Not feeling well because of illness.

doloroso—painful

Causing or feeling physical pain.

sin aliento—breathless

Having difficulty breathing after effort or illness.

hinchado—swollen

Larger than normal because of injury or irritation.

con picor—itchy

Having a skin feeling that makes one want to scratch.

mareado—dizzy

Feeling as if the body or surroundings are spinning.

con náuseas—nauseous

Feeling as if vomiting may happen.

cansado—tired

Needing rest because energy has been used.

hambriento—hungry

Feeling a need for food.

sediento—thirsty

Feeling a need for water.

soñoliento—sleepy

Feeling a desire or need to sleep.

despierto—awake

Not sleeping and aware of the surroundings.

débil—weak

Having little physical strength or energy.

fuerte—strong

Having plenty of physical strength.

pálido—pale

Having unusually little color in the face or skin.

sudoroso—sweaty

Covered with or producing sweat.

limpio—clean

Free from dirt or harmful substances.

sucio—dirty

Covered with dirt or unclean substances.

en forma—fit

In good physical condition for activity.

flexible—flexible

Able to bend and move easily.

rígido—stiff

Hard to bend or move freely.

entumecido—numb

Unable to feel touch normally in a body part.

consciente—conscious

Awake and aware of oneself and the surroundings.

inconsciente—unconscious

Not awake or aware of the surroundings.

To clean the body or a body part with water.

enjuagar—rinse

To use clean water to remove soap or dirt.

escupir—spit

To force saliva or another substance out of the mouth.

pellizcar—pinch

To squeeze skin or a small body part between the fingers.

peinar—comb

To make hair neat by passing a comb through it.

bañarse—bathe

To wash the whole body in water.

secar—dry

To remove water from the body, hair, or skin.

desinfectar—disinfect

To clean a place or wound to reduce harmful germs.

cubrir—cover

To place clean material over a wound for protection.

envolver—wrap

To wind material around an injured body part.

presionar—press

To push steadily on a body area with the hand or fingers.

frotar—rub

To move the hand back and forth over the skin.

rascar—scratch

To move the nails across itchy skin.

aplicar—apply

To spread medicine or cream over the skin.

inhalar—inhale

To draw air into the lungs.

exhalar—exhale

To send air out of the lungs.

bostezar—yawn

To open the mouth wide and take a deep breath when tired or sleepy.

ronquido—snoring

A rough breathing sound made while sleeping.

temblar—shiver

To shake slightly because of cold, fear, or illness.

desmayarse—faint

To lose consciousness for a short time.

sanar—heal

To become healthy again after illness or injury.

fortalecer—strengthen

To make the body or a body part stronger through repeated activity.

relajarse—relax

To let the body and mind become calm and less tense.

acostarse—lie down

To place the body flat on a bed or other surface.

levantarse—get up

To rise from a bed or a lying position.

Stage 6

enfermedad—illness

A condition that prevents the body or mind from working normally.

dolor—pain

An unpleasant feeling caused by injury or a problem in the body.

fiebre—fever

A fever means the body’s temperature has risen above its normal range.

resfriado—cold

A common mild illness that often affects the nose and throat.

tos—cough

A sudden action that forces air out through the throat.

estornudo—sneeze

A sudden burst of air through the nose and mouth.

dolor de cabeza—headache

Pain felt in the head.

dolor de estómago—stomachache

Pain felt in the belly.

dolor de muelas—toothache

Pain felt in or around a tooth.

herida—wound

An area where the skin or flesh has been damaged.

moretón—bruise

A dark mark under the skin caused by a hit or pressure.

quemadura—burn

An injury caused by heat, fire, or a hot substance.

hinchazón—swelling

An enlarged body area caused by injury or irritation.

picazón—itch

A skin feeling that makes a person want to scratch.

sarpullido—rash

A group of red or irritated spots that appears on the skin.

cicatriz—scar

A lasting mark left after a wound has healed.

mareo—dizziness

A feeling that the body or surroundings are spinning or unsteady.

náusea—nausea

An uncomfortable feeling that may come before vomiting.

fatiga—fatigue

A strong lack of physical or mental energy.

hambre—hunger

The feeling that appears when the body needs food.

sed—thirst

The feeling that appears when the body needs water.

recuperación—recovery

The process of becoming healthy or strong again.

lesión—injury

Physical damage caused by an accident, fall, or impact.

infección—infection

A condition caused when harmful germs enter and multiply in the body.

sangrado nasal—nosebleed

Bleeding that comes from inside the nose.

Stage 7

intestino—intestine

The long organ that digests food and carries away waste.

columna vertebral—spine

The line of bones supporting the center of the back.

costilla—rib

One of the curved bones protecting the chest organs.

cráneo—skull

The hard bones surrounding and protecting the brain.

articulación—joint

The place where two bones meet and movement can occur.

vena—vein

A blood vessel that carries blood back toward the heart.

arteria—artery

A blood vessel that carries blood away from the heart.

nervio—nerve

A thin body structure that carries signals between the brain and body.

sudor—sweat

Liquid released through the skin when the body is hot or active.

lágrima—tear

Liquid that comes from the eyes during emotion or irritation.

saliva—saliva

Liquid in the mouth that moistens food and helps digestion.

aliento—breath

Air taken into and released from the lungs.

voz—voice

Sound produced by a person when speaking or singing.

vista—sight

The ability of the eyes to see clearly.

oído—hearing

The ability of the ears to detect sounds.

olfato—smell

The sense that detects odors through the nose.

gusto—taste

The sense that detects flavors in the mouth.

tacto—touch

The sense that detects contact, pressure, and texture through the skin.

temperatura corporal—body temperature

The degree of warmth inside a person's body.

pulso—pulse

The regular beat felt as the heart moves blood.

altura—height

The measurement from the bottom of the feet to the top of the head.

peso—weight

How heavy a person's body is.

fuerza—strength

The physical ability to push, pull, lift, or endure.

energía—energy

The physical and mental power to be active.

salud—health

Health is the state of the body and mind working well.

Stage 8

medicina—medicine

A substance used to treat illness or reduce symptoms.

hospital—hospital

A place where sick or injured people receive medical care.

clínica—clinic

A small medical place where people receive examination and treatment.

farmacia—pharmacy

A place that prepares and sells medicine.

médico—doctor

A person trained to find and treat health problems.

enfermero—nurse

A person who cares for patients and supports medical treatment.

paciente—patient

A person receiving medical examination or treatment.

ambulancia—ambulance

A vehicle that quickly carries sick or injured people for medical care.

venda—bandage

A strip of material wrapped around an injured body part.

curita—adhesive bandage

A small sticky covering placed over a minor wound.

termómetro—thermometer

A tool used to measure body temperature.

inyección—injection

Medicine given into the body through a needle.

tratamiento—treatment

Medical care given to improve an illness or injury.

examen—examination

A medical check to understand a person's condition.

diagnóstico—diagnosis

The identification of an illness from signs and examination.

cirugía—surgery

Medical treatment performed by operating on part of the body.

receta—prescription

A medical instruction stating which medicine a patient should use.

emergencia—emergency

A dangerous situation that requires immediate action.

primeros auxilios—first aid

Immediate basic care given before full medical treatment is available.

cuidado—care

Help given to protect someone's health and comfort.

prevención—prevention

Action taken to stop illness or injury before it happens.

higiene—hygiene

Practices that keep the body and surroundings clean and healthy.

nutrición—nutrition

Substances from food that help the body grow and function.

hábito—habit

A behavior repeated regularly until it becomes usual.

chequeo médico—checkup

A health examination performed to find problems early.

Stage 9

digestión—digestion

The process of breaking food down so the body can use it.

apetito—appetite

The natural desire to eat food.

inmunidad—immunity

The body's ability to resist harmful germs and disease.

crecimiento—growth

The process of becoming larger and more developed.

pubertad—puberty

The period when a child's body develops toward adulthood.

menstruación—menstruation

The regular release of blood from the uterus as part of the reproductive cycle.

presión arterial—blood pressure

The force of blood pushing against the walls of blood vessels.

circulación—circulation

The continuous movement of blood around the body.

postura—posture

The way a person holds the body while sitting, standing, or moving.

equilibrio—balance

The ability to keep the body steady without falling.

somnolencia—sleepiness

The feeling of wanting or needing to sleep.

deshidratación—dehydration

Dehydration happens when the body loses more water than it takes in.

obesidad—obesity

A condition of having an unhealthy amount of body fat.

insomnio—insomnia

A condition that makes it difficult to fall asleep or remain asleep.

fractura—fracture

A break or crack in a bone.

esguince—sprain

An injury caused when a joint is twisted too far.

intoxicación—poisoning

Illness caused by a harmful substance entering the body.

asfixia—choking

A dangerous condition in which breathing is blocked.

diarrea—diarrhea

A condition involving frequent loose or watery bowel movements.

estreñimiento—constipation

Constipation makes bowel movements difficult or less frequent than usual.

vómito—vomiting

The forceful emptying of stomach contents through the mouth.

sangrado—bleeding

The loss of blood from a damaged blood vessel.

inflamación—inflammation

The body's response that can cause redness, heat, swelling, or pain.

calambre—cramp

A sudden tightening of muscles that cannot be easily controlled.

conciencia—consciousness

The state of being awake and aware of oneself and the surroundings.

Stage 10

síntoma—symptom

A change in the body or mind that may show illness or injury.

asma—asthma

A condition that can narrow the airways and make breathing difficult.

diabetes—diabetes

A disease in which the body has difficulty controlling blood sugar.

anemia—anemia

Anemia leaves the blood unable to carry enough oxygen around the body.

cáncer—cancer

A disease in which abnormal cells grow without proper control.

neumonía—pneumonia

An illness in which the lungs become inflamed, often making breathing difficult.

hipertensión—high blood pressure

High blood pressure means blood pressure stays above a healthy range.

hipotensión—low blood pressure

Low blood pressure means blood pressure is below the usual range.

caries—cavity

A damaged area of a tooth caused by decay.

golpe de calor—heatstroke

A dangerous illness caused by the body becoming too hot.

quemadura solar—sunburn

Skin damage caused by too much sunlight.

congelación—frostbite

Damage to body tissue caused by extreme cold.

mareo por movimiento—motion sickness

Nausea or dizziness caused by movement during travel.

miopía—nearsightedness

A vision condition in which nearby objects are clearer than distant ones.

hipermetropía—farsightedness

A vision condition in which nearby objects may appear unclear.

daltonismo—color blindness

A condition that makes some colors difficult to tell apart.

pérdida auditiva—hearing loss

A partial or complete reduction in the ability to hear sounds.

enfermedad contagiosa—contagious disease

An illness that can spread from one person or living thing to another.

enfermedad crónica—chronic disease

An illness that lasts for a long time or repeatedly returns.

desnutrición—malnutrition

Poor health caused by receiving too little or unbalanced nutrition.

sonambulismo—sleepwalking

Sleepwalking is getting up or moving around while still asleep.

pérdida de memoria—memory loss

The loss of some or all memories.

convulsión—seizure

A sudden episode in which body movement or awareness becomes uncontrolled.

accidente cerebrovascular—stroke

A serious condition caused when blood flow in the brain is blocked or a vessel bursts.

cálculo renal—kidney stone

A hard mass that forms from substances in the urinary system.
