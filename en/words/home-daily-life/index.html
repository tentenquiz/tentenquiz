Home / Daily life Word Dictionary

The home and daily life section starts with the spaces inside a house, like room, door, and window, then expands into the people around where you live, like neighbour, tenant, and landlord, as the stages go up. It moves from describing the layout of a home to describing your relationships with the people who share that space. This page organises the words from TentenQuiz's learning data by stage, in one glossary. You can see the exact words and definitions that appear in the quiz, so it is useful for skimming before you play or reviewing words you have already learned.

Each stage has 25 words, starting with easier words in Stage 1 and moving to slightly more advanced expressions as the number increases. The small text under a word is a reading aid for your learning language.
